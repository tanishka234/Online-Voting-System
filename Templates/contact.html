Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register to vote?

Click on the "Register" button on the homepage and fill in your details. You'll need to provide a valid email address and create a password. After registration, you can log in and verify your voter ID to cast your vote.

Is my vote secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and security measures. Each vote is anonymized and you receive a unique receipt number to verify your vote was counted correctly.

Can I change my vote after submitting?

No, votes cannot be changed once submitted to maintain the integrity of the election process. Please review your choice carefully before confirming.

What should I do if I face technical issues?

Contact our technical support team via the contact form above or email us at support@votingsystem.com. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.
