Step-by-Step Guide

Account Registration

Create your account using your valid email address and secure password. Verify your email to activate your account.

  • Click on "Register" in the navigation bar
  • Fill in your details: Name, Email, Password
  • Agree to terms and conditions
  • Click "Register" and verify your email

Login to System

Access your account using your registered credentials.

  • Click "Login" in the navigation bar
  • Enter your email and password
  • Complete any additional security verification if prompted
  • You'll be redirected to the home page

Voter Verification

Verify your identity using your voter ID and personal information.

  • Click "Vote Now" button on the home page
  • Enter your Voter ID and full name
  • Allow location access or enter manually
  • System verifies against registered voter database

Cast Your Vote

Select your preferred candidate in the secure voting interface.

  • View all candidates with party symbols
  • Read candidate manifestos
  • Click on your preferred candidate
  • Review your selection and confirm

Receive Digital Receipt

Get instant confirmation of your vote with a digital receipt.

  • System generates unique receipt number
  • Download or print your receipt
  • QR code for verification
  • All details stored securely
